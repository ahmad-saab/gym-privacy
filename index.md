# Privacy Policy — Gym

**Updated:** 2026-10-26
**Contact:** sales@ftservices.ae

## The short version

- Everything you log is saved in a database on your phone. There are no accounts,
  no servers, no ads and no analytics. We never see your data.
- Data leaves your phone only if you choose to use AI, and then only to the AI
  provider you picked.

## Stored on your phone

- Workouts, sets, exercises, programs and records.
- The profile you enter: training type, goal, experience, equipment, injuries or
  limitations, height, birth year, sex, body weight and body-fat %.
- Your profile photo, as a copy in the app's private folder.
- Each day's active calories read from Health Connect, if you connect it.
- AI history, the companion's memory, and your AI key in Android's encrypted storage.
- Android backup is off for this app, so none of it is copied to your Google account.

## AI (optional)

- AI is off until you add your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter or
  Ollama Cloud.
- It runs only when you tap an AI button. Then a short summary of your training goes
  straight from your phone to that provider. It never passes through us.
- The summary can include recent sessions and sets, muscles trained, your training
  type, goal, experience, equipment, injuries or limitations, body weight and any
  text you type.
- The provider handles it under its own privacy policy and terms, and bills usage to
  your account. Your key is sent only to that provider.

## Health Connect (optional)

- If you connect it, the app reads your calories burned (active and total calories
  records) and saves each day's active calories in the app's database on your phone,
  to show your activity on the Calories card.
- It only reads from Health Connect and never writes to it. The saved numbers stay
  on your phone and are never sent anywhere, including to AI.
- Health data is never used for advertising, or for employment, insurance or credit
  decisions.

## Exports

- CSV and Excel files are made only when you tap Export, and go only where you share
  them.

## Permissions

- Internet: AI requests only.
- Notifications and exact alarms: the rest timer alert.
- Health Connect: reading calories burned.

## Deleting your data

- You → Delete all my data erases workouts, programs, profile, body entries, daily
  activity calories, weekly targets, AI history, AI memory, AI settings, your AI key
  and your profile photo.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the phone.
- Health Connect access can be turned off in Health Connect's settings.

## Children

The app is not meant for children under 13. The profile does not accept a birth year
under 13.

## Health information

The app is a training log. It is not a medical device and does not give medical
advice. Calorie figures are estimates.

## Changes

This page is updated if the app's data handling changes, with the new date at the top.
